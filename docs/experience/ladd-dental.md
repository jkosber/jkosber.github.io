# Case Study: Multi-Site Healthcare IT Support

**Organization:** Ladd Dental Group  
**Role:** IT Intern  
**Location:** Kokomo, IN (12 Practice Locations)  
**Duration:** November 2025 – May 2026  
**Scope:** 100+ Clinical & Administrative Endpoints, 12 Remote Clinics  

---

## Operational Context

Ladd Dental Group operates 12 dental practices across North-Central Indiana. Each clinic relies on high-availability workstation endpoints for clinical imaging, digital charting, scheduling, and billing. The objective of this role was to provide rapid-response Tier 1/Tier 2 support, execute workstation refresh cycles, and maintain hardware asset integrity.

Central IT in Kokomo managed the 12 clinics remotely via Level RMM and ClickUp, with on-site staging for deployments and decommissions.

---

## Core Responsibilities & Technical Work

### 1. Help Desk & Remote Fleet Administration
* Handled an average of **20+ weekly support tickets** generated by doctors, hygienists, and front-desk staff across all 12 locations.
* Monitored endpoint health, background processes, and system availability using **Level RMM**.
* Performed remote troubleshooting for driver crashes, network share disconnects, specialized dental imaging software errors, and peripheral failures.
* Logged detailed symptoms, root causes, remediation steps, and follow-up notes in **ClickUp** to maintain knowledge-base consistency.

### 2. Workstation Staging & Domain Deployment
* Staged, configured, and deployed **30+ new Windows workstations** across the practice network.
* Executed standard deployment checklist:
  * Operating system initialization and Windows Update baseline patching.
  * Joining workstations to the corporate **Active Directory** domain.
  * Installing and validating clinical software suites, imaging drivers, and network printers.
  * Delivering workstations onsite, executing physical cable management, and verifying end-user readiness.

### 3. Secure Hardware Decommissioning
* Managed the retirement and decommissioning of **50+ aging desktop workstations**.
* Followed organizational security procedures to ensure all local storage drives were properly sanitized before disposal.
* Maintained a strict **documented chain of custody** tracking device serial numbers, asset tags, retirement dates, and disposal methods.

## Key Scenarios & Problem Resolution

??? example "Scenario: Imaging Sensor Disconnect Across Operatories"
    * **Issue:** Several operatories at an affiliate clinic reported intermittent sensor disconnects during active patient examinations.
    * **Troubleshooting:** Remotely analyzed USB controller power-management settings via Level RMM and device manager. Identified that aggressive Windows USB selective suspend settings were powering down imaging sensors after short idle intervals.
    * **Resolution:** Reconfigured power-saving profiles via group policy and device settings, resolving the disconnect issue across all clinic operatories.

??? example "Scenario: Domain Authentication Failure Post-Migration"
    * **Issue:** Newly deployed front-desk workstation failed to authenticate domain credentials after being delivered to a remote satellite clinic.
    * **Troubleshooting:** Verified local network link, tested DNS resolution against the primary domain controller, and identified an incorrect DNS server IP assigned by a local clinic router DHCP scope.
    * **Resolution:** Corrected the clinic DHCP option settings to point to the primary internal DNS server, re-authenticated the domain trust relationship, and validated user logon.
