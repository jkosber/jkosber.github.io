# Healthcare IT support

**Ladd Dental Group · IT Intern · November 2025–May 2026**

I supported 12 dental offices and 100+ clinical and clerical staff, handling help desk requests, workstation deployments and onsite connectivity problems.

## Responsibilities

- Resolved 20+ weekly tickets involving workstations, peripherals, printers and network connectivity, using Level RMM and onsite visits.
- Recorded symptoms, troubleshooting steps, resolutions and follow-up notes in ClickUp.
- Deployed and configured 30+ workstations, including hardware setup, software installation, domain joining and user readiness support.
- Decommissioned 50+ aging workstations that could not support Windows 11, following the organization's procedures and documenting chain of custody.
- Installed switches, access points and routers, and pulled, terminated and tested a switch-to-switch Ethernet run extending a fiber uplink.

## Cabling repair

I traced a failed wired connection from the wall jack back to the patch panel. Reterminating the keystone restored connectivity.
